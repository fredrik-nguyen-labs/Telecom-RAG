Responsible use

Terms and limitations

Telecom RAG is an experimental educational and research tool, not an operator-grade network monitoring or fault-management system. Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Acceptable use

Use the demo for exploration, study and general telecom analysis. Do not submit private subscriber information, network credentials, personal data or confidential measurements. Avoid automated abuse, scraping or attempts to bypass request limits.

Accuracy and engineering decisions

AI-generated answers and source selection may contain mistakes. Dataset-relative statistics are not universal network thresholds and do not demonstrate causation. Validate technical claims against standards, actual network measurements and qualified engineering judgment before operational use.

Availability

The demo is provided on a best-effort basis and may be slow or temporarily unavailable due to hosting limits, maintenance, third-party providers or changes to the public dataset. There is no service-level guarantee.

Data and source rights

External datasets, specifications and technical documents remain subject to their respective owners' rights and licenses. Source links are provided for provenance. The project's own source code licensing is documented in its public GitHub repository.

Privacy

Read the privacy notice before using the interactive assistant.
